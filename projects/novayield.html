01 / Climate & agriculture

NovaYield

A farm-adaptation decision-support prototype exploring crop-rotation options with NASA Earth observations, local soil information, crop characteristics, and farmer priorities.

Contribution
Backend engineering & team leadership
Status
In development; backend partly implemented
Selected stack
FastAPI, Python, MongoDB, React / Vite

The context

Changing temperature and rainfall, water shortages, extreme weather, and declining soil health make farm planning more complex. NovaYield explores placing NASA Earth observations alongside local soil information, crop characteristics, and farmer priorities.

What the prototype explores

The concept supports exploration of crop-rotation options and trade-offs, to inform consideration and discussion around farm adaptation.

My contribution

I contribute backend engineering and team leadership. The backend is partly implemented, and the frontend is still under development.
